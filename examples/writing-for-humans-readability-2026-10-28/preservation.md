## Keep the meaning through the edit

After rewriting, compare the copy with its sources. Check who acts on what, numbers and units, negation, eligibility, timing, comparisons, uncertainty, and exceptions. Keep each condition attached to the claim it limits, and keep the same term for the same concept. Explain a necessary term rather than replacing it with a broader claim. Leave an unknown actor unknown instead of inventing one to make a sentence active.

Check that a reader can still recover every material answer the source provided. Restore a lost qualification or causal link before shipping, including after the final scrub. A smoother sentence is not a successful edit if its meaning changed.
