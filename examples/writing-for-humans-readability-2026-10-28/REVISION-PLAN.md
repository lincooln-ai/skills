## Decision from the registered pilot

B retained 72/72 source-grounded answers, R 71/72, F 70/72 and RF 71/72. R/F/RF each had at least one source-scope or actor problem. Reject the original two additions as a combined production change. Their small clarity score differences do not override the retention gate.

Keep a narrower, conditional source-comparison pass instead. It asks for an internal actor/action/condition inventory before editing, preserves the direction of a conditional, and checks the final text after the scrub. The local reading repair permits explicit connecting words without prescribing global sentence splitting or banning constructions. This is an exploratory response to observed failures, not the winning arm of the registered factorial experiment.

Reuse en-onboarding, en-release, en-technical, zh-comparison and en-voice-control from the registered set. They are unseen by the revised writer, but their design is informed by those failures; they are not an independent benchmark.

All 14 outputs must preserve each critical relationship and condition to pass this regression check. A separate fresh judge reviews the outputs with sources; a reviewer then inspects the flagged text and concrete source relationships. This check does not compare the final revision's average performance with B, and cannot establish superiority or a human reading benefit.

Keep the original pilot immutable. Store revision prompts, responses, metadata and results under revision/. If a regression fails, record it and make a labeled correction rather than replacing an earlier output.
